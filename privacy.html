Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Applies to the Haltly app for Android and iOS.

In short: Haltly collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything it knows stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall it.

What Haltly uses on your phone

To do its job, Haltly works with this information on your device only:

  • The apps you choose and the rules you set (delays, daily limits, blocks, schedules).
  • Which app is open, so it can show its pause or block screen at the right moment.
  • How long you use apps, to show your screen time and apply daily limits.
  • Your sessions and settings, such as how many minutes you chose.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. There is no server.

Permissions

Android

  • Usage access: to know which app is open and how long apps are used.
  • Display over other apps: to show the pause and block screens over the app you opened.
  • Notifications: for "5 minutes left" and "unlock ready" alerts, and the ongoing "Haltly is on" notice.
  • Alarms and starting after reboot: so sessions, blocks and schedules keep working when Haltly is closed.
  • Accessibility (optional, "Precision mode"): only if you turn it on, to notice the instant a chosen app opens. It reads only the name of the app in front, never what is on the screen or what you type.

iOS

  • Screen Time: Apple's Screen Time framework lets Haltly shield the apps you choose and show your usage. Apple keeps app identities private: Haltly receives only opaque tokens, and usage numbers are shown inside Apple's own report view.
  • Notifications: for session and unlock alerts.

What we collect

Nothing. Haltly includes no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and the Android app does not request internet access.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not have any.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays in Haltly's storage on your phone until you delete it. Use Settings › Delete everything in the app, or uninstall Haltly; both remove it completely. Haltly turns off device backups for its data, so it is not copied to cloud backups. Settings › Export my data saves a copy as a file only when you ask.

Children

Haltly is meant for people aged 13 and over. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Because Haltly collects nothing, any change will not affect data we hold about you: there is none.
